# PAANI POP — Venture Charter & 12-Month Business Plan (India)

> **Document status:** Working venture charter and validation plan. This is a planning document, not an approved investment case.
**Working name:** PAANI POP (not trademark-cleared).
**Market:** India first. **Initial format:** 500 ml water carton, subject to regulatory and supplier validation.
**Planning horizon:** 23 November 2026 – 22 November 2027.
**Business model:** Brand owner and distribution network; outsourced water production and filling.

---

# 1. Executive summary

PAANI POP is a proposed advertising-funded free-water and brand-activation network. Consumers receive packaged drinking water at no charge. Advertisers fund the product, packaging, campaign creative and distribution in exchange for physical brand exposure and optional digital engagement.

The venture is not intended to compete as a conventional retail water brand. Its product is a sponsored, measurable physical-media campaign delivered through a useful everyday product.

**Core proposition:** Free water for people. A distinctive, measurable activation channel for brands.

**Initial wedge:** Sponsored 500 ml cartons distributed through banquet halls, weddings, corporate events and selected campus or community events in one Indian launch cluster.

**Operating principle:** Own the consumer brand, advertiser relationships, creative system, distribution partnerships and campaign reporting. Outsource water sourcing, treatment, filling and carton manufacture to appropriately licensed partners.

**Critical launch condition:** Do not commercially commit to the carton format until written regulatory advice and manufacturer documentation confirm that the exact packaging, product, filling process and labelling are compliant in India.

# 2. Project charter

## 2.1 Purpose
Validate whether Indian advertisers will repeatedly pay enough to fund compliant, quality-assured free drinking water distributed through trusted venues, with a differentiated creative and interactive packaging experience.

## 2.2 Vision
Build a network of useful, free-to-consumer products that connect brands with people through creative physical experiences. Water is the first product and the initial distribution wedge.

## 2.3 Mission
Make brand-funded hydration accessible to consumers while giving advertisers a distinctive, accountable way to reach audiences in real-world settings.

## 2.4 Problem
- Consumers encounter large volumes of interruptive digital and conventional advertising.
- Event venues need dependable guest amenities and may welcome sponsored supply if it does not undermine their positioning.
- Advertisers seek memorable, targeted activations with evidence of delivery and engagement.
- Conventional water packaging is usually functional rather than designed as an interactive media surface.

These are hypotheses to validate through interviews, not established market findings.

## 2.5 Proposed solution
A branded 500 ml carton with:
- A consistent PAANI POP identity.
- One primary sponsor or a small, non-conflicting sponsor set.
- A creative campaign message.
- An optional QR interaction or offer.
- All mandatory food, product, manufacturer, batch and legal declarations.
- A campaign identifier for distribution and reporting.

## 2.7 Scope

**In scope for year one**
- Brand and positioning validation.
- Packaging and regulatory feasibility.
- Contract-manufacturer sourcing.
- Advertiser discovery and sales.
- Venue and event distribution partnerships.
- QR campaign landing pages and basic analytics.
- Pilot production and fulfilment.
- Campaign reporting and repeat-sales validation.

**Out of scope initially**

- National distribution.
- Retail supermarket distribution.
- Building a complex self-serve advertising marketplace.
- Expanding into tissues, cups, snacks or other sponsored products before water economics are proven.
- Making unsupported environmental, health, purity or social-impact claims.

# 3. Business model and value exchange

## 3.1 Two-customer model
- **Paying customer:** Advertiser, brand, agency or local business.
- **End consumer:** Person who receives the water free of charge.
- **Distribution partner:** Venue, event organiser, banquet hall, college, office or other approved location.
- **Production partner:** Licensed manufacturer responsible for the agreed water production and filling process.

## 3.2 Value propositions

| Stakeholder | Value offered |
| --- | --- |
| Consumer | Free, safe, quality-assured drinking water; entertaining packaging; optional useful interaction |
| Advertiser | Sponsored physical placement, audience/location selection, creative execution and campaign reporting |
| Venue | Sponsored water allocation, convenient fulfilment and optional venue-specific editions |
| Manufacturer | Repeat contract orders and predictable production schedules |
| PAANI POP | Campaign revenue, advertiser relationships, venue network and reusable campaign capabilities |

## 3.3 Revenue streams
1. **Sponsored Drop** — sponsor funds a defined production and distribution batch.
2. **Venue Takeover** — one advertiser receives exclusive campaign placement at a venue or event.
3. **Shared Sponsorship** — two or three non-competing advertisers share campaign economics and packaging space.
4. **QR Experience** — paid interactive landing page, quiz, offer, redemption or campaign mechanic.
5. **City or multi-venue campaign** — distribution across a defined set of locations.
6. **Custom edition** — premium design, event-specific or limited-run packaging.

Pricing should be quoted per campaign, separating production, distribution, creative, media and digital components.

## 3.4 Payment policy
- Signed campaign order and approved artwork before production.
- Collect advance covering committed production and logistics costs.
- No speculative inventory.
- Report cartons produced, delivered and distributed separately from scans or engagement.

# 6. MVP definition

## 6.1 MVP statement
Provide one sponsored batch of compliant 500 ml water cartons, distributed through two or three partner venues, with a distinctive creative design, one optional QR experience and a post-campaign delivery and engagement report.

## 6.2 MVP boundaries
- **Must have:** Approved product/packaging spec, licensed contract manufacturer, launch cluster, 2-3 venue partners, sponsor advance, creative system, QR destination, batch records, regulatory sign-offs.
- **Not in MVP:** Consumer app, self-serve dashboard, loyalty points, national delivery.

| Shared sponsorship lowers advertiser entry | Carton MOQ and supply disruption |

# 8. Unit economics and financial model

## 8.1 Base-case assumption
- Filled carton: ₹32
- Inbound transport: ₹2
- Venue handling: ₹2
- QR and technology: ₹1
- Contingency: ₹1
- **Total variable cost:** **₹38**
- **Average sponsor revenue:** ₹50 per carton
- **Contribution:** ₹12 per carton (24% margin)

## 8.2 Campaign scenarios
- 1,000 cartons: ₹50,000 revenue | ₹38,000 cost | ₹12,000 contribution
- 2,000 cartons: ₹1,00,000 revenue | ₹76,000 cost | ₹24,000 contribution
- 5,000 cartons: ₹2,50,000 revenue | ₹1,90,000 cost | ₹60,000 contribution
