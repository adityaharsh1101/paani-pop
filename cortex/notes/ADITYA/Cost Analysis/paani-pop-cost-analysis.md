# PAANI POP — Bottle Cost Analysis & Sponsor Rate Card

**Model date:** 25 November 2026  
**Working geography:** Delhi NCR, India (pilot assumption; not a final market decision)  
**Product modelled:** 500 ml custom-labelled PET packaged drinking water bottle  
**Operating model:** PAANI POP owns advertiser, campaign and venue relationships; a qualified third-party manufacturer fills and packs the water; PAANI POP manages sponsorship, artwork coordination, distribution and reporting.

> This is a planning model, not a supplier quotation, tax opinion or guarantee of profit. The earlier 500 ml carton concept remains a separate format option. This analysis specifically follows the user's latest request for bottles. Do not compare the carton and PET cost assumptions as if they were the same product.

## 1. What the market quotes currently suggest

Public supplier pages reviewed on 25 November 2026 show the following 500 ml references:

| Supplier | Public 500 ml price reference | Published minimum / relevant terms |
|---|---:|---|
| AquaVia | ₹5.25/bottle at its enterprise tier; ₹5.67 list | Minimum 145 cases × 24 = 3,480 bottles; supplier page says GST and transport are included |
| ORA Water | ₹168/24 = ₹7.00 one-time; ₹156/24 = ₹6.50 at 300+ boxes; ₹120/24 = ₹5.00 contract | Custom-label minimum 200 boxes × 24 = 4,800 bottles; freight billed at actuals; page says GST included |

| Spenca | ₹155/24 = ₹6.45 square bottle; ₹200/24 = ₹8.33 premium bottle | Minimum 500 boxes × 24 = 12,000 bottles; vendor page says listed prices include 5% GST |

**Model input:** ₹6.50 per bottle as the base purchase-cost assumption, with a low case of ₹5.25 and a high case of ₹8.33. This is not a confirmed PAANI POP procurement rate. The quotes are not perfectly like-for-like: bottle shape, label coverage, pack quality, tax claims, freight and contractual terms differ. Obtain written, comparable quotes before accepting a sponsor order.

| Creative and campaign admin | ₹1,000 / ₹1,500 / ₹2,000 | Internal cost allowance by package |

A 500 ml bottle weighs about 0.5 kg of water before packaging. Therefore 1,000 bottles represent at least 500 kg of water and 10,000 bottles at least 5,000 kg (5 tonnes), before cartons and pallets. A bike courier is not a realistic bulk-delivery solution. Use contracted commercial vehicles, confirm payload/legal limits, and separately plan loading, unloading, event access and leftover-stock collection.

## 5. Supplier-price sensitivity at 10,000 bottles

At the proposed ₹13.50 per bottle plus ₹5,000 setup fee:

| Supplier price | Contribution before shared overhead | Margin before shared overhead | After ₹20,000 shared overhead |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ₹5.25 | ₹43,600 | 31.1% | ₹23,600 |
| ₹6.50 | ₹30,850 | 22.0% | ₹10,850 |
| ₹8.33 | ₹12,184 | 8.7% | (₹7,816) |

The supplier price is a key profit driver. The commercial target should be a comparable custom-label quote at or below approximately ₹6.50 per bottle before locking sponsor rates. A premium ₹8.33 bottle needs a higher sponsor rate or a separate premium package.

## 6. Monthly operating run-rate

Assumes each 10,000-unit campaign is sold at the 10k rate card, one delivery/dispatch associate is employed for the month, and shared monthly overhead is ₹20,000. Transport, unload and creative/admin costs scale with each 10k campaign.

| Monthly bottles | Net sponsor revenue before GST | Total estimated costs incl. one staff member and shared overhead | Operating profit before income tax |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10,000 | ₹140,000 | ₹129,150 | ₹10,850 |
| 20,000 | ₹280,000 | ₹213,000 | ₹67,000 |
| 30,000 | ₹420,000 | ₹296,850 | ₹123,150 |

**Hiring advice:** outsource delivery and event-day labour for the first pilots. Consider a full-time delivery/dispatch associate only once recurring work is at least around 20,000 bottles/month or multiple recurring campaigns make that salary productive. These run-rate profits are still before founder pay, income tax, finance costs, insurance, major rent, sales commission and any marketing spend above the ₹20,000 overhead placeholder.

## 7. Working capital and payment terms

- Ask the sponsor for an advance that covers the full non-cancellable supplier MOQ, freight reservation, artwork/printing and event-day commitments. The model tests an 85% advance, but the 1,000-unit reusable-stock scenario still has a ₹6,649 funding gap because the supplier MOQ forces an initial inventory buy.
- For bespoke sponsor labels at 1,000 units, do not begin production under a 1,000-unit price if the supplier's MOQ is 2,400. Price the full MOQ or refuse the job.
- Collect the balance before dispatch or under a contractually defined delivery milestone.
- Put unused stock ownership, artwork reuse, event cancellation, delayed delivery, rejects, recall, damaged stock and refund obligations in the sponsor contract.
- Do not book production until cleared funds cover the agreed commitment.

## 8. Tax, quality and product release cautions

The CBIC rate schedule lists heading 2201 waters (other than drinking water packed in 20-litre bottles) at 18% in the schedule reviewed. Some supplier pages describe their prices as including 5% GST. Those claims are inconsistent with the general CBIC schedule shown and must not be copied into a PAANI POP quote without a CA confirming the exact HSN, product classification, bundled supply treatment and invoice structure. The spreadsheet displays 18% as a placeholder only; tax is excluded from profit calculations.

FSSAI's official testing scheme for packaged drinking water and mineral water is effective from 1 January 2026. FSSAI's December 2025 order notes that mandatory BIS certification is no longer required for packaged drinking water/mineral water, while the testing scheme and records must be complied with. Verify the manufacturer's current FSSAI licence, facility, product/batch testing, source, traceability, label and recall process. Do not treat a supplier's website statement as sufficient evidence.

## 9. Costs not fully included / still need quotes

- Product testing, independent lab reports, regulatory/technical review and artwork legal review.
- Product liability, stock, transit and event insurance.
- Warehouse rent or long-term storage, forklift/pallet equipment and major handling needs.
- Sales commissions, paid advertising and founder salary.
- Legal setup, accounting, tax advisory, bank/payment fees and working-capital interest.
- QR analytics provider fees, CRM/email costs and any app infrastructure beyond the current basic allowance.
- Multiple delivery drops, waiting time, tolls, parking, night work, remote sites and post-event returns.
- Deposit, printing plates, custom mould, artwork reprint or minimum order deposit if charged by selected supplier.

## 10. Immediate quote request checklist

Send the same RFQ to at least three qualified suppliers and require:

1. Exact 500 ml bottle specification, PET weight, cap and tamper evidence, case pack and pallet/carton dimensions.
2. Price at 1,000 / 2,400 / 3,480 / 4,800 / 10,000 bottles, with custom-label and stock-label options shown separately.
3. MOQ, lead time, advance, cancellation, refundable deposits and ownership of unused printed stock.
4. Ex-GST price, HSN, GST rate, tax invoice and whether freight is included or billed separately.
5. Facility identity, current FSSAI licence, testing scheme records, batch certificates, traceability and recall process.
6. Delivery address, payload, vehicle type, loading/unloading, waiting time, transit insurance and proof of delivery.
7. Written confirmation that all mandatory product declarations remain visible and compliant after sponsor branding.
