# PAANI POP — Instagram: 30 Days of Content

**Campaign posture:** concept and pilot planning. Product packs shown are illustrative mockups. Do not imply live distribution, certified product, confirmed supply partners or campaign results.

**Voice:** optimistic, grounded, slightly playful, clear about the model. Use a visual-first edit; keep on-image copy minimal. All packaging images should carry a small 'concept artwork' or 'illustrative' label until approved.
